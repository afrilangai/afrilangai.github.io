Sponsors & Partners

Organizing Committee

General Chair

Dr. Ernest Mwebaze
Executive Director, Sunbird AI

Executive Director of Sunbird AI. Over a decade of research at Makerere University (co-founding the AI Lab), former research scientist at Google AI Ghana and UN Global Pulse Kampala. PhD from University of Groningen.

Co-Chairs

Dr. Richard Kimera
Lecturer, Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST)

Focuses on NLP and Neural Machine Translation for low-resource languages, including the English–Luganda parallel corpus. PhD from Handong Global University.

Dr. Joyce Nakatumba-Nabende
Head of AI Lab, Makerere University

Senior Lecturer and Head of Makerere AI Lab, leading initiatives in NLP, Machine Learning, and Responsible AI across East Africa. PhD from Eindhoven University of Technology.

Program Committee Chairs

Dr. Andrew Katumba
Lecturer, Makerere University

Head of the Marconi Research and Innovation Lab at Makerere University, applying AI to healthcare in low-resource environments. PhD from Ghent University.

Dr. John Quinn
Director of Research, Sunbird AI

Director of Research at Sunbird AI, former Staff Research Software Engineer at Google (Open Buildings project) and UN Global Pulse. PhD from University of Edinburgh.

Prof. Engineer Bainomugisha
Chair of Computer Science, Makerere University

Professor & Chair of Computer Science at Makerere University, founder of AirQo, and founding board member of Sunbird AI. PhD from Vrije Universiteit Brussel.
